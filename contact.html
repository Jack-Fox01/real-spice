Allergy information

Most of our dishes contain garlic, soya, gluten and sesame oil. Some also contain milk, eggs, wheat, peanuts, tree nuts, fish and shellfish.

If you have an allergy, please phone us on 01333 422533 before you order.

About Real Spice

Real Spice is an Indian takeaway on Shorehead in Leven, Fife, open every evening from 4pm until midnight for collection and delivery.

The menu runs from Indian curries, tandoori dishes and set meals to kebabs, pizza and burgers.
